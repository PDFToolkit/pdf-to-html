# Deriving HTML 1.2: clause decisions and evidence

[English](requirements.md) · [Français](fr/requirements.md)

This ledger concerns the retained PDF Association usage specification, not a
claim of complete ISO 32000-2, Acrobat or browser implementation. Page numbers
are physical PDF pages, including the six-page front matter.
[Open the original](../resources/reference-documents/Deriving_HTML_from_PDF_1-2.pdf).

**Native** means executable package behavior. **Policy** means an explicit
security decision permitted by the reference. **Port** means the package
prepares data and calls an application-owned capability. **HTML adaptation**
means the literal PDF rule conflicts with current HTML/browser behavior and the
decision is recorded with diagnostics. Tests exercise concrete cases; a table
entry alone does not prove universal conformance.

This is the 0.6.0 decision matrix for all numbered clauses and annexes of the
retained 74-page reference. Grouped rows cover the complete stated range; tables
are expanded where their individual choices matter. Clauses 2–3 define terms and
notation, and annexes A–D provide implementation guidance; they are not separate
conversion engines. Bibliography is on physical page 74.

## Complete table inventory and permitted choices

| Table / pages | Inventory and decision |

| 4 / 47–50 | Placement, WritingMode, BackgroundColor, BorderColor/Style/Thickness, Padding, Color, SpaceBefore/After, StartIndent/EndIndent, TextIndent/Align, TPadding, LineHeight, BaselineShift, TextDecorationColor/Thickness/Type and RubyAlign/Position are mapped; TextPosition creates sub/sup. | 9 / 63 | HTML/XHTML, CSS, JavaScript, JPEG/PNG/GIF, SVG and embedded MathML selection is implemented under the associated-file policy. Document splitting, inferred ARIA/URL text, note/reference popups, view zoom,
GoToR/GoToE integration and list continuation engines are optional or
implementation-dependent. They are not claimed as native universal behavior.
Safe associated-file restrictions are permitted by §4.6.1. A raster engine,
full PDF ECMAScript API, certificate trust/revocation policy and receiving-viewer
permission enforcement remain explicit application responsibilities. HTML-valid
adaptations are listed in [coverage](coverage.md); they are not literal
reproductions of incompatible PDF-to-HTML examples.
